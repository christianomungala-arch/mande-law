Emmanuel Mumia

About Emmanuel

Emmanuel Mumia is the founder and managing partner of M & E Advocates, based at The Pavilion, Westlands, Nairobi. An advocate of the High Court of Kenya, Emmanuel brings sharp legal acumen, strategic thinking, and a deep commitment to client service.

Since establishing M & E Advocates in 2025, Emmanuel has built a reputation for delivering clear, practical, and results-driven legal advice to individuals, entrepreneurs, and businesses across Nairobi and beyond.
