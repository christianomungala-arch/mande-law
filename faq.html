Property & Real Estate

A title search confirms that the property you are buying is legally owned by the seller, is not subject to any liens or encumbrances, and that the title is genuine. It is a critical step before any property purchase in Kenya.

A straightforward property transfer typically takes 30 to 60 days depending on the Land Registry's workload. Our conveyancing team actively follows up to ensure your transfer is completed as quickly as possible.

Family Law

Under the Matrimonial Property Act 2013, property is divided based on each spouse's contribution — financial or otherwise. Courts consider direct financial contributions, domestic work, and child-rearing when determining each party's share.

Kenyan courts always prioritize the best interests of the child. Factors considered include the child's age, the parent's ability to provide care, stability of the home environment, and the child's own wishes if they are old enough to express them.
