Our Story

M & E Advocates was founded in 2005 by senior advocates Margaret Muthoni and Edwin Omondi with a shared vision — to provide world-class legal services rooted in integrity, honesty, and genuine care for clients.

Starting from a modest office in Westlands, Nairobi, the firm has grown into one of East Africa's most respected law firms with over 12 advocates, two offices, and a track record of over 1,500 successfully resolved cases.

Today M & E Advocates serves individuals, businesses, NGOs, and government institutions across Kenya and the wider East African region.
